# Maven Toy Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance for a toy retail business. The aim is to understand revenue, profitability, order volume, and product category performance over time.
## Power BI Report Preview

![Maven Toy Store Sales Report](images.png)

## Business Objectives

- Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Compare order volume and revenue across product categories.
- Identify changes in sales performance over time.
- Develop data-driven recommendations to support business decisions.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data modeling and table relationships
- Data visualization and business analysis

## Data Model

The project uses four tables:

- **Sales:** Transaction-level sales information.
- **Products:** Product attributes, prices, and costs.
- **Stores:** Store information.
- **Calendar:** Date information for time-based analysis.

The tables were imported into Power BI and organized into a relational data model.

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Orders
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit

## Analysis and Findings

The analysis compared sales performance between December 2022 and January 2023.

- **Overall performance:** Total revenue declined from approximately $877,000 to $747,000, a decrease of 14.8%.
- **Toys:** Revenue declined by approximately 17.0%, from $264,000 to $219,000. Orders also decreased from 10,000 to 9,000.
- **Sports & Outdoors:** Revenue declined by approximately 31.0%, from $129,000 to $89,000.
- **Arts & Crafts:** Revenue increased by approximately 2.1%, from $191,000 to $195,000.
- **Electronics:** Revenue remained relatively stable, declining by approximately 2.8%, from $108,000 to $105,000.

These results highlight differences in category-level performance and identify areas for further investigation.
